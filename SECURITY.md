# Security

Report vulnerabilities privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/codingducksrl/ciesign-react-native/security/advisories/new), not in public issues. Fixes go into the latest release only.

In scope: anything that exposes the PIN or the saved PIN, sends the PIN to a card other than the one it was saved for, signs without the PIN or a biometric, or signs something other than what the app passed.

Never include a real PIN, PUK, card certificate or signed personal document in a report.
